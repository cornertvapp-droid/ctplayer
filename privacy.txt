1. Information used to play videos
CT Player plays video links entered by you or received from another application. It may process the video URL, content identifier, optional title, playback mode, and playback state to open and control the requested video.
The app does not require an account or maintain a local viewing-history database. Video URLs and related input may remain in memory while the app is running. Native playback components may temporarily cache media or generate diagnostic information.

2. Connections to video providers and other services
Playing a video connects your device to the media provider and any delivery servers used by that provider. These services may receive your IP address, requested URL, access tokens included in the URL, request headers, and technical connection information.
For content opened through supported match links, CT Player sends the content identifier to a provider service to record an opening attempt and retrieve available streams. An opening may be recorded even if playback does not start or complete.
The app also contacts an update service before playback. Some streams require a connection to the ipify public-IP service to prepare access credentials. On iOS, the app may contact Apple to locate its App Store listing when you select Update.
External services process information according to their own practices and privacy policies. This includes the provider of the optional sample video.

3. Advertising through Google AdMob
CT Player uses Google AdMob to display full-screen advertisements before playback.
Depending on the platform, permissions, consent choices, and SDK configuration, Google’s advertising services may process:
- IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them.
- Device, app, and advertising identifiers where available.
- Ad impressions, interactions, and other app interaction information.
- Diagnostic and performance information.
This information supports advertising delivery, measurement, service operation, and fraud prevention. Google describes these practices in its Android and iOS SDK disclosures.
CT Player requests non-personalized ads. These ads still involve data processing. The app does not deliberately include your video URL or video title in advertising requests.
Google’s handling of personal information is described in its Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising privacy choices
CT Player uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to determine whether advertising requests are permitted and to present applicable privacy choices.
Where available, you can revisit these choices through About → Ad privacy choices. The app skips advertising requests when the consent SDK does not permit them. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that lawfully occurred before withdrawal.
You can also use advertising privacy controls provided by your device. Non-personalized advertising does not necessarily eliminate consent requirements. See Google’s consent documentation.

5. Firebase and connection recovery
CT Player uses Google Firebase Remote Config to obtain replacement API connection settings when provider requests repeatedly fail.
Firebase may process an installation identifier, IP address, app information, and technical service information to deliver and operate this configuration service. CT Player does not intentionally send video URLs or titles to Remote Config.
This version does not integrate Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. Firebase’s practices are described in its Privacy and Security documentation.

6. Information stored on your device
CT Player stores certain operational settings locally, including saved API hostnames, the selected hostname, connection-failure state, and an advertising/admin-mode preference. Advertising SDKs may also store privacy choices and other operational information.
These settings are separate from viewing history. You can remove local app data through your operating system’s controls. Removing the app or clearing local data does not necessarily delete records already held by external services, and operating-system backups may retain or restore some settings.
The app reads clipboard text when you select Paste and writes a generated link when you select a copy action.
CT Player does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photo library, or precise device location. Your IP address may nevertheless reveal an approximate location to connected services.
